AIRTIME-23 · Future feature screens — consent, feedback, AI suggestions

Capture, review, suggest

Activity capture is opt-in, visible and revocable. The review screens give the member the same view of captured data the system holds, and AI suggestions are always reviewed before they become entries. No consent means no capture and no disabled controls pretending otherwise.

Consent and permissions

Each signal type is a separate, reversible permission. Declining leaves the feature off, not broken.

Future-feature notes

States covered

  • Consent grant, decline and revoke, each per signal type.
  • Capture active, paused and error.
  • Suggestion high, medium and low confidence.
  • Deletion with anonymisation confirmation.

Privacy

  • No consent is a clean empty state, never a dead disabled screen.
  • Body content, screenshots and message contents are out of scope.
  • Low confidence never auto-creates an entry.

Accessibility

  • Confidence is shown as a word, a meter and a percentage.
  • Switches have explicit labels and no colour-only meaning.
  • The deletion receipt is announced as a status message.
