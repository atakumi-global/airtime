AIRTIME-21 · Project and budget screens

Cost, budget, price, profit and margin

Projects and clients are owned by Plane and arrive read-only; Airtime adds the financial layer on top. Five numbers describe every project, two of them have their own targets owned by different people, and every threshold is shown as an icon plus a word, never colour alone.

The financial model

Cost
The actual cost of the team and freelancers based on time spent, at each person's preset cost level.
Budget
The required resources valued at their commercial rates. This is the resource budget the delivery team works within.
Price
What we sell the project to the client for.
Profit
Price minus actual budget used.
Margin
Available budget minus Cost.

Two targets, two owners

Profit and margin are deliberately separate because different groups are accountable for them. Airtime tracks both per project and never blends them into one health score.

Profit target - sales / accountSet against Price. Answers "is this deal worth having?"
Margin target - delivery / financeSet against Budget. Answers "are we delivering within our means?"

Flow and accessibility

End-to-end flow

  • Set a budget and a price on a Plane project.
  • Set cost levels and commercial rates.
  • See Cost, Budget, Price, Profit and Margin update.
  • Set a profit target (sales) and a margin target (delivery).

Data states

  • No budget shows a call to action; no price blocks Profit with a clear reason.
  • Sync error falls back to cache, marked as cached.
  • Stale FX is shown with the rate date and a retry.

Accessibility

  • Every threshold is an icon plus a word plus a number.
  • The chart has a text alternative summarising Cost, Budget, Price and projection.
  • Disabled manager controls stay focusable with a reason.
