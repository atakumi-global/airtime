Usage guidance

Do

  • Use one primary button per screen.
  • Keep control height at 32px (26px small) for Plane-like density.
  • Give every field a visible label, not placeholder-only.
  • Pair colour with an icon or word for any status.

Don't

  • Use the danger red for anything reversible.
  • Put destructive actions in the primary slot.
  • Rely on the pulse animation to signal running.
  • Show a spinner without also disabling the control.

Accessibility notes

  • Dialogs trap focus, restore it on close, and close on Escape.
  • Toasts use role status or alert and are never the only feedback.
  • Focus rings are 2px with offset on every interactive element.
  • Targets are at least 24x24px; icon buttons are 26-32px.
